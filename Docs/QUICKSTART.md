# Personal TaskBar – Quick Start Guide

Get up and running in under 5 minutes.

---

## 1. First Launch

Double-click **Personal_TaskBar.exe**.

A thin bar appears at the top of your screen containing one sample section called **Getting Started** with a link to this guide. The bar floats above all other windows and stays visible while you work.

---

## 2. Add Your First Section

1. Click the **+** button in the control strip (the narrow band at the very top of the bar).
2. Type a name for your section (e.g. *Dev Tools*) and press **Enter** or click **Add**.

A new section appears with a blue accent line beneath its name.

---

## 3. Add an Entry to the Section

1. Right-click the section header (e.g. *Dev Tools*).
2. Choose **Add Entry**.
3. Fill in the dialog:
   - **Name** – what you want to call this shortcut (e.g. *VS Code*)
   - **Type** – choose `exe`, `folder`, `file`, or `url`
   - **Path / URL** – browse for a file/folder or type a URL
   - **Icon Override** – leave blank to auto-detect the icon
4. Click **OK**.

The entry icon appears in your section. **Left-click it to launch.**

---

## 4. Resize the Bar

Drag the bottom edge of the bar (or the right edge if the bar is vertical) to resize it. All icons, labels, and spacing scale automatically based on the **Icon Size** setting.

For precise control, click **⚙ Settings** and use the **Icon Size** slider (24–96 px).

---

## 5. Dock the Bar to a Screen Edge

Drag the bar toward any screen edge. When you get within 20 pixels, the bar snaps and stretches to fill that edge. Drag it back towards the centre to undock.

Supported dock positions: **Top**, **Bottom**, **Left**, **Right**.

The bar remembers its dock position and monitor between restarts.

---

## 6. Use Search

Press **Ctrl+Space** (or click the 🔍 button) to open the search bar.

- Start typing to filter entries live across all sections.
- Press **Enter** to launch the top match.
- Press **Escape** to close search and restore the normal view.

---

## Next Steps

- **Right-click a section header** to rename it, change its accent colour, switch display mode (icons only / icons + labels / labels only), or convert it to a **Scratchpad** for quick notes.
- **Right-click an entry** to edit it, move it to another section, open its file location, or remove it.
- Open **⚙ Settings** to configure startup behaviour, always-on-top, and custom hotkeys.
- See [MANUAL.md](MANUAL.md) for a complete feature reference.
